# Backend Engineer

**Company:** Cloud Harbor (fictional)
**Location:** Remote (Latin America)
**Type:** Full-time

## About the role

We are looking for a Backend Engineer to design and maintain the services that
power our logistics platform. You will work closely with product and frontend
teams.

## Responsibilities

- Build and maintain REST APIs with Node.js and TypeScript.
- Design PostgreSQL schemas and queries.
- Deploy and monitor services on AWS.
- Improve CI/CD pipelines and containerized environments.
- Participate in code reviews and incident response.

## Requirements

- 3+ years of backend development experience.
- Node.js and TypeScript.
- PostgreSQL.
- AWS.
- Docker.
- Experience with REST APIs and automated testing.
- Strong written communication in English.

## Benefits

- Remote work and annual learning budget.
- Health insurance.
