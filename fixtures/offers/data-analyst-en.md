# Data Analyst

**Company:** Northwind Logistics (fictional)
**Location:** Remote (United States)
**Type:** Full-time

## About the role

We are looking for a Data Analyst to join our operations team. You will turn
shipment and inventory data into dashboards and recommendations for the
operations and finance teams.

## Responsibilities

- Build and maintain dashboards for delivery performance and inventory.
- Analyze trends and explain variances to non-technical stakeholders.
- Automate recurring reports and document data definitions.
- Partner with operations managers to define and track KPIs.

## Requirements

- 3+ years of experience in data analysis.
- Advanced SQL.
- Tableau for dashboard development.
- Python for data cleaning and automation.
- Advanced Excel.
- Strong written communication in English.

## Benefits

- Health insurance and annual learning budget.
- Fully remote within the United States.
